### R01

#### Response

### Characterization of the Category "I feel able not to take the plane"

The category "I feel able not to take the plane" is characterized by a strong sense of capability in avoiding airplane travel, with a notable distinction in how restrictive this behavior is perceived compared to the full sample. Key observations include:

1. **Perception of Plane Avoidance as Non-Restrictive**:
   - A substantial majority (76.54%) in this group view not taking the plane as non-restrictive, which is significantly higher than the full sample (37.13%).
   - Conversely, only 23.46% perceive it as restrictive, which is lower than the full sample (54.49%).
   - This suggests that this group feels more empowered and less burdened by the decision to avoid air travel.

2. **Contrasting Views on Restrictiveness**:
   - While the group overwhelmingly sees not taking the plane as non-restrictive, a smaller but notable portion (23.46%) still views it as restrictive, indicating some internal diversity in perceptions.

3. **Other Environmental Practices**:
   - The group shows a slightly higher reluctance to let washing air dry instead of using a tumble dryer, with 12.35% feeling unable to do so compared to 7.78% in the full sample.
   - They also exhibit a higher tendency to perceive buying only locally sourced food as restrictive (25.93% vs. 19.16% in the full sample), suggesting a nuanced approach to other environmental practices.

4. **Quantitative Mean Differences**:
   - The mean score for the restrictiveness of not taking the plane is lower in this group (1.68 vs. 2.68 in the full sample), reinforcing the perception of non-restrictiveness.
   - Interestingly, the mean score for the restrictiveness of turning off appliances is higher (1.93 vs. 1.65 in the full sample), indicating that while this group feels capable of avoiding planes, they may find other small environmental actions slightly more burdensome.

### Distinguishing Features
This group stands out primarily due to their strong capability and positive perception of avoiding airplane travel, which is less restrictive for them compared to the broader population. However, their views on other environmental practices are more varied, with some practices perceived as more restrictive. This suggests that while they are confident in their ability to avoid planes, their attitudes toward other sustainable behaviors are not uniformly less restrictive.
