The category "I feel able not to take the plane" is characterized by a strong perceived capability to avoid plane travel, particularly without feeling restricted by this decision. Key features of this group include:

1. **Perceived Non-Restrictiveness of Avoiding Plane Travel**:
   - A large majority (76.54%) of respondents in this group feel able not to take the plane and perceive this as non-restrictive, which is significantly higher than the full sample (37.13%).
   - Conversely, only 23.46% of this group feel able not to take the plane but perceive it as restrictive, which is still higher than the full sample (11.38%). This suggests that while some in this group find the decision restrictive, most do not.
   - The overall perception of "never plane is not restrictive" is much higher in this group (76.54%) compared to the full sample (45.51%), while the perception of "never plane is restrictive" is much lower (23.46%) compared to the full sample (54.49%).

2. **Lower Mean Perception of Restrictiveness for Avoiding Plane Travel**:
   - The mean score for the restrictiveness of avoiding plane travel ("From 0 to 5 never plane is restrictive") is lower in this group (1.68) than in the full sample (2.68), indicating that this group generally views the avoidance of plane travel as less restrictive.

3. **Mixed Perceptions on Other Environmental Practices**:
   - **Linen Care**: A higher proportion of this group (12.35%) feels unable to let their washing air dry rather than use a tumble dryer and perceives linen care as restrictive compared to the full sample (7.19%). Additionally, this group is less likely (87.65%) to feel able to let their washing air dry compared to the full sample (92.22%), and more likely (12.35%) to feel unable to do so compared to the full sample (7.78%).

   - The mean score for the restrictiveness of turning off appliances ("From 0 to 5 turn off is restrictive") is higher in this group (1.93) than in the full sample (1.65), suggesting that this group finds turning off appliances more restrictive than the general population.

### Summary
The category "I feel able not to take the plane" is distinguished by a strong and predominant belief that avoiding plane travel is non-restrictive, supported by both qualitative-frequency and quantitative-mean evidence. While this group generally views avoiding plane travel as feasible and unrestrictive, they exhibit mixed perceptions on other environmental practices, particularly around linen care and local food sourcing. Additionally, they perceive turning off appliances as more restrictive compared to the full sample. This suggests a nuanced approach to environmental practices, where the ability to avoid plane travel is a standout characteristic, but other behaviors may present varying levels of difficulty or restrictiveness.
