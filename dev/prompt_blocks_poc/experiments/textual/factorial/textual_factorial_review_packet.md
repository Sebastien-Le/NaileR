This packet is blind. No condition mapping is displayed. No assessment box is pre-checked.

## CONTEXT

The study examines how respondents evaluate fabric samples for a possible car interior, including touch, appearance, material impression, practicality, and perceived quality.

## QUESTION

Reconstruct the group's discourse as a coherent configuration. Identify recurring frames and internal tensions, while preserving the modality of the texts: opinions, tentative impressions, and practical constraints should not be restated as universal facts.

   - Observations on fabric texture, mesh, and natural feel.
   - *Representative Texts*: [TXT000003] ("fine fabrics and small meshes"), [TXT000002] ("good for the leather").

#### **Higher-Level Interpretation**
The group's discourse reflects a tension between **practical concerns** (durability, maintenance) and **aesthetic preferences** (discreet, natural, sophisticated). While some respondents prioritize functionality, others emphasize visual and tactile appeal. The fabric's perceived quality is a recurring theme, with opinions varying on whether it meets expectations for a car interior.

#### **Internal Diversity**
- **Practical vs. Aesthetic Priorities**: Some focus on durability ([TXT000001], [TXT000006]), while others emphasize appearance ([TXT000005], [TXT000007]).
- **Sophistication vs. Simplicity**: Views differ on whether the fabric is sophisticated enough ([TXT000004] vs. [TXT000008]).

#### **Tension Texts**
- [TXT000001] ("not thick enough") vs. [TXT000008] ("qualitative aspect") – *Tension between perceived durability and quality*.
- [TXT000004] ("not sophisticated enough") vs. [TXT000005] ("discreet without being too unpleasant") – *Tension between sophistication and simplicity*.

#### **Literal vs. Interpreted Expression**
- **Literal**: Respondents express individual opinions on touch, appearance, and practicality.
- **Interpreted**: The group's discourse reveals a balancing act between functionality and aesthetics, with no consensus on the fabric's overall suitability for a car interior.

This profile preserves the modality of the texts, acknowledging that opinions and impressions vary rather than stating universal facts.
