# TEXTUAL ablation 2 evidence fidelity review

Candidate transformations are extracted mechanically from modality-related wording. No transformation is pre-judged.

## CONTEXT

The study examines how respondents evaluate fabric samples for a possible car interior, including touch, appearance, material impression, practicality, and perceived quality.

## QUESTION

Reconstruct the group's discourse as a coherent configuration. Identify recurring frames and internal tensions, while preserving the modality of the texts: opinions, tentative impressions, and practical constraints should not be restated as universal facts.
