## Sources and route

`/Users/sebastienle/Documents/2023-2024/MASHS/quality/Analyse_logos.R`
reads `perception de la qualité 2024.xlsx`, removes the first two rows, and
uses nine 0–10 logo ratings. The script then runs PCA, HCPC with three groups
(15, 8 and 32), `catdes()`, three `nail_condes()` calls on PCA dimensions and
one `nail_catdes()` call on the groups.

The package `quality` object is 55 × 9 and numerically matches the documented
logo-score structure. The package help now expresses the offline CONDES route
through `nail_condes(generate = FALSE)`, `nail_evidence()` and
`nail_prompt()`, with the real backend call kept in `\dontrun{}`.
