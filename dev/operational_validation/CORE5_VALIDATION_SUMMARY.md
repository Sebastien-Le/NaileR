# CORE5 validation summary

Date: 2026-10-28

This document summarizes the first operational validation cycle for the five
core workflows. It is a preservation and orientation record, not a definitive
scientific validation of any LLM interpretation.

| Workflow | Operational status | Main preserved cases | Scientific status |
|---|---|---|---|
| CATDES | successful | Iris, waste, local food, atomic habits and transition examples | preliminary; grounding remains required |
| CONDES | successful | observed and constructed targets, including ACM dimensions and Distinction | preliminary; associations are descriptive, not independent validation tests |
| DESCFREQ | successful | Beards, Children and Rorschach | preliminary; frequency evidence does not establish personality, causes or preferences |
| QDA | successful | Chocolates, Strawberries, Apples, Perfumes and Melons | preliminary; sensory narratives require evidence-by-evidence grounding |
| TEXTUAL | successful | Fabric, Car Alone, Atomic Habits, Rorschach and Distinction | preliminary; technical parsing does not validate semantic claims |

## Common technical controls

The campaigns preserved deterministic previews, exact prompts, canonical
evidence, raw responses and relevant accessors. Where structured parsing was
available, parser status and referenced identifiers were checked separately
from scientific interpretation. Generation examples remain opt-in and must
not be confused with ordinary offline package examples.

## Cross-workflow scientific limits

The five workflows expose associations, profiles, dimensions, frequencies or
textual material. They do not automatically justify causal explanations,
normative rankings, consumer preferences, observed behaviour or psychological
claims. Concepts added by an LLM can be useful hypotheses, but they must be
traced to the supplied evidence before scientific use.

The operational archives and state records remain the authoritative sources
for case-level counts, parameters, hashes and limitations. Private or
individual-level material is kept outside the public package corpus.

## Use for grounding and teaching

The corpus is suitable for a progressive R-Ladies demonstration: deterministic
data preparation, evidence inspection, exact-prompt inspection, optional
generation, and human review of claims. The next step is grounding selected
claims to their canonical markers or text IDs, not treating successful API
calls or parseable output as a scientific endpoint.
